02 / How to think

DSA is not memorization.

Learn a repeatable way to attack an unfamiliar problem.

👀

Understand

Read the problem. Identify input, output, rules, constraints and edge cases.

🧠

Think

Start with a simple brute-force idea. Then ask: what repeated work can I remove?

⚙️

Optimize

Choose the data structure or pattern that reduces unnecessary work.

04 / Suggested execution

Study in stages.

Do not try to finish the whole syllabus in a few days. Build the foundation first.

STAGE 01

Foundation

Programming basics, logic building, patterns, complexity and sorting.

STAGE 02

Core DSA

Arrays, hashing, binary search, strings, recursion and linked lists.

STAGE 03

Patterns

Bits, greedy, sliding window, two pointers, stacks and queues.

STAGE 04

Advanced

Trees, BST, heaps, graphs, DP, tries, advanced strings and maths.

05 / Golden rules

Remember these.

1️⃣

Don't copy immediately

Try the problem yourself first. Even a wrong attempt teaches you where your thinking breaks.

2️⃣

Always know complexity

After solving, ask: “How much time? How much extra space?”

3️⃣

Revision is mandatory

Re-solve difficult problems later without looking at the old code.
